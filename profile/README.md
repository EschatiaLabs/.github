# Eschatia Labs

Eschatia Labs is an independent, non-profit open-source AI research lab. We publish our preprints on
[eschatialabs.com](https://eschatialabs.com), our models on [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/eschatialabs)
and our code here and under [mertkayacs](https://github.com/mertkayacs).

What we work on:

- Deepfake detection: [xdfdet](https://github.com/mertkayacs/xdfdet), explainable frame-level detection.
- Decision models: [JevAlt](https://github.com/mertkayacs/jevalt) and [JevOss](https://github.com/mertkayacs/jevoss), small models that pick an answer and say how sure they are.
- Local AI and open tools: [Tholos](https://github.com/mertkayacs/tholos) and [reevesagents](https://github.com/mertkayacs/reevesagents).

Founded by [Mert Kaya](https://mertkayacs.com) and Betül Keleş Kaya.
